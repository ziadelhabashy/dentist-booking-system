Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Sara Galal is a highly skilled and compassionate dentist with over 15 years of experience in general and cosmetic dentistry. She graduated with honors from Cairo University Faculty of Dentistry and completed her Master's in Cosmetic Dentistry at King's College London.

Dr. Sara believes that every patient deserves personalized care in a comfortable, welcoming environment. Her gentle approach and attention to detail have earned her the trust of thousands of patients across Cairo.

When she's not transforming smiles, Dr. Sara enjoys staying at the forefront of dental innovation through continuous education and attending international dental conferences.
